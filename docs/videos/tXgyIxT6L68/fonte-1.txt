Sources - Campi Flegrei: Strongest Earthquake in 40 Years (Critical Latitude)
This video is educational reporting on an ongoing seismic sequence at Campi Flegrei, Italy. It is not a prediction that an eruption is imminent. Every figure below is linked to its primary source.
