The Krakatoa tsunami of December 22nd, 2018 killed more than 400 people around Indonesia's Sunda Strait, and no giant eruption warned anyone it was coming.

Anak Krakatau, the island that grew inside the caldera left by the 1883 collapse, dropped its entire southwestern flank into the sea that night. The rock displaced the water, and the waves reached coasts on Java and Sumatra. That same volcano is erupting again now, and the question people keep asking is whether what we are watching is moving it toward another tsunami.
