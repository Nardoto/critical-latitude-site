CHAPTER 02

YOUR GO-BAG

One bag, packed now, by the door you'd actually leave from. Use a wheeled bag or backpack you can genuinely carry. If it's too heavy to lift, it's packed wrong.

DOCUMENTS & MONEY

  • Photocopies of ID, Medicare/insurance cards, and prescription list (in a zip-top bag)
  • Copies of deed/lease, insurance policies, and key contacts (or a USB drive with scans)
  • Cash in small bills — ATMs and card readers fail with the power
  • Spare house and car keys

HEALTH — PACK THIS SECTION FIRST

  • 7-day supply of every medication, in original labeled bottles if possible
  • Written medication list: drug, dose, schedule, prescriber (worksheet in Ch. 12)
  • Spare eyeglasses (an old prescription beats none)
  • Hearing aid batteries or charger — a week's worth
  • Spare cane / walking aid if you use one
  • Denture care, incontinence supplies, any daily medical items
  • First aid kit, hand sanitizer, N95 masks

WHEN DISASTER STRIKES

FRIDGE CARD

EARTHQUAKE — SHAKING STARTS

  1. DROP — or sit and lean forward
  2. COVER head & neck
  3. HOLD ON until it stops

TORNADO WARNING

  1. Interior room, lowest floor
  2. Away from windows
  3. Cover your head

FLOOD / WATER ON ROAD

  1. Don't drive into water
  2. Move to high ground

BEFORE YOU WALK ANYWHERE

  • Sturdy shoes ON (glass, debris)
  • Flashlight — never candles
  • Smell gas? Get out, don't flip switches

THEN

  • TEXT (don't call): “I'm OK, I'm at ___”
  • Radio ON for instructions
  • Grab go-bag if leaving
  • Generator OUTDOORS only — 20 ft from windows
